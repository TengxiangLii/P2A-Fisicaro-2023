## Supplementary information of: Principles of isomer stability in small clusters

Giuseppe Fisicaro<sup>∗</sup>

_Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche, Istituto per la Microelettronica e Microsistemi (CNR-IMM), Z.I. VIII Strada 5, I-95121 Catania, Italy_

Bastian Schaefer, Jonas A. Finkler, and Stefan Goedecker

In the following we show two additional analysis concerning the redistribution of the electronic charge density for selected Na and Mg clusters together with various figures from the main publication augmented by the data used to make the figure plus selected structural and electronic properties.

[Supplementary Figure 1](../assets/supp_figs/supplementary-figure-1.jpg)

FIG. S1. Difference between the selfconsistent DFT charge density and the sum of the atomic charge densities of spherically symmetrized atoms. For each iso-value only the positive part is shown which illustrates the flow of the electronic charge density when the structure is formed out of individual atoms. All representations refer to an isovalue of 0.00032. Top row shows the charge difference for bulk Na and the global minimum of the Na<sub>8</sub> cluster. Bottom panels in the second row show the charge difference for the global minima of the Na<sub>10</sub> and Na<sub>15</sub> clusters. For large identical iso-charge values, the volume included by the iso-surface is always substantially larger in the case of the clusters compared to the solid. Since these regions of high charge densities are in between neighboring atoms, the bonds are contracted in the cluster compared to the solid.
